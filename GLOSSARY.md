# DebtWise

DebtWise decides whether money is better spent prepaying a Brazilian home loan now or held in
Brazilian treasury bonds and used to prepay later. Portuguese names follow each term in parentheses.

## Mortgage

**Mortgage** (financiamento imobiliário):
A home loan repaid in monthly installments under an amortization system, whose balance is corrected
by an indexer. Its conditions are inputs, never assumptions.
_Avoid_: Loan, financing, debt

**Amortization System** (sistema de amortização):
The rule that splits each installment into amortization and interest, such as SAC or Price.

**Indexer** (indexador):
The index that corrects the outstanding balance before interest is charged, such as TR or the
poupança yield.

**Poupança Yield** (remuneração da poupança):
The legally defined savings-account return, TR plus a Selic-dependent spread, used as an indexer.

**Outstanding Balance** (saldo devedor):
The principal still owed on the mortgage, after indexer correction.
_Avoid_: Debt, principal

**Installment** (prestação):
One scheduled monthly payment: amortization, interest, insurance premiums and fees.
_Avoid_: Payment, parcel

**Prepayment** (amortização extraordinária):
A payment against the outstanding balance outside the schedule, which either shortens the term or
lowers the following installments.
_Avoid_: Early payment, fast-track, paydown

**Avoided Cost**:
The indexer correction, interest, insurance and fees a prepayment saves between its date and a later
date.
_Avoid_: Interest saved, juros

**Statement** (extrato):
The outstanding balance the bank reports on a date, which overrides the computed balance from then
on.

## Investments

**Bond** (título):
A Tesouro Direto security DebtWise can compare against a prepayment: Tesouro Prefixado (LTN) or
Tesouro Prefixado com Juros Semestrais (NTN-F).
_Avoid_: Investment, asset, título público

**Lot**:
One purchase of a bond: its date, contracted rate, unit price and quantity.
_Avoid_: Investment, position, aporte

**Coupon** (cupom):
A semiannual interest payment made by an NTN-F before maturity.

**Accrual Value** (valor na curva):
A lot's value compounded at its contracted rate up to a date.

**Market Value** (valor a mercado):
A lot's value at the bond's current sell price.

**Maturity Value** (valor no vencimento):
A lot's value when its bond matures.

**Custody Fee** (taxa de custódia):
The B3 fee charged on bonds held in Tesouro Direto.

## Comparison

**Decision**:
A forward-looking comparison of prepaying a given amount on a given date against buying a bond with
it.

**Early Sale Check**:
A comparison, for a lot already held, of selling it today and prepaying with the proceeds against
holding it to maturity and prepaying then.
_Avoid_: Early redemption, resgate antecipado

**Horizon**:
The date a comparison is measured on: the maturity of the bond being compared.

**Equal Outflow**:
The rule that both compared scenarios pay the same out of pocket every month, with any freed cash,
including coupons, prepaid on the day it frees up.

**Advantage**:
The difference in net worth on the horizon between investing and prepaying; positive favours
investing.

**Break-even Rate**:
The gross annual bond rate at which the advantage is zero.

**Index** (índice):
A published Brazilian economic series that drives a mortgage or an investment, such as TR, IPCA or
CDI.

**Business Day** (dia útil):
A day in the ANBIMA calendar on which bond and CDI interest accrues.
